# Case Study: Enterprise Schedule Integrity Audit & Risk Mitigation

## 1. The Problem
A multi-million-dollar capital project portfolio was facing chronic schedule slippage, opaque critical paths, and misaligned contractor forecasting. Executive leadership lacked a transparent, data-driven mechanism to audit baseline logic and identify latent float suppression before milestone delivery dates were compromised.

## 2. The Technical & Operational Constraints
- **Data Fragmentation:** Schedules were siloed across disparate contractor formats with inconsistent activity coding structures.
- **Logic Distortion:** High usage of open-ended logic loops, negative lag, and excessive soft constraints masked true project risks.
- **Reporting Lag:** Manual variance reporting took weeks, preventing proactive intervention.

## 3. The Solution
Designed and deployed a rigorous project controls framework combined with automated script-driven schedule diagnostics:
1. **Baseline Health Diagnostics:** Established automated DCMA 14-point schedule health assessments to catch logic errors instantly.
2. **Critical Path Reconstruction:** Standardized Work Breakdown Structures (WBS) and streamlined logic links across all contractor submittals.
3. **Executive Dashboarding:** Integrated structured data pipelines to deliver real-time risk exposure metrics directly to senior leadership.

## 4. The Quantifiable Result
- **Predictability Gain:** Eliminated over 85% of hidden logic anomalies within the first audit cycle.
- **Time Savings:** Reduced schedule review and audit cycles from 10 days down to under 48 hours.
- **Cost Avoidance:** Prevented estimated multi-week project delays by surfacing critical path resource bottlenecks ahead of execution windows.
